## Verse 1
They say I’m worthless, but for You I am precious  
They say all of my flaws, but You see an image of Yours  
In Your words, I find my peace  
So I turn to You Jesus, not the Crowd

## Verse 2
They count all my wrongs, but Your mercy leads me home  
They think I’m on my own, but Your spirit calms the storm  
In Your Word, I find my peace  
So I turn to You Jesus, not the crowd
